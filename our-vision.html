Our Vision

La famiglia comes first

Sicily III is a family owned restaurant. The people who make your food are the same people who answer the phone, and we take that seriously.

Our Passion

In our own words

As a family owned small business, we depend on the satisfaction of our customers. We aim to meet every one of our customers' needs and wants. With that being said, we provide the best quality of food and service that our employees can perform.

We've been providing exceptional value for almost 17 years. On this route, we plan on providing the same results for many years to come.

From the Alps to central Italy to the island of Sicily. Welcome to Sicily Restaurant, where you will enjoy original recipes.

The route our recipes take

A trip down the boot, one plate at a time

The North

Rich, comforting cooking. Think layered lasagna, cheese tortellini and soft gnocchi.

Central Italy

The Roman classics, like Saltimbocca Romana, chicken cacciatore and creamy fettuccini Alfredo.

Sicily

Where our name comes from. Thick square pies, eggplant parmigiana, Marsala wine sauce and cannoli.
