Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information the Sicily III Restaurant & Pizza website ("we," "us") collects, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it simple: we collect very little, we never sell it, and we only use it to run the restaurant.

What we collect

  • Mailing list. If you sign up, we collect your name and email address so we can send you updates and discounts.
  • Catering requests. If you send a catering request, we collect your name, phone number, email, event date, guest count and any notes you write, so we can plan your order and call you back.
  • Basic technical data. Like every website, our hosting provider automatically receives standard request information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. This is used to keep the site running and secure.

We do not ask for payment information on this website. We do not use advertising trackers, and this site does not set cookies of its own.

How we use it

  • To send the emails you signed up for.
  • To respond to catering requests and questions.
  • To keep the site secure and working properly.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

Services we rely on

  • Vercel hosts this website and processes basic request logs.
  • Web3Forms delivers form submissions to our inbox.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on this site, which means your browser requests font files from Google.
  • Google Maps is only loaded if you click "Show the map." Until then, nothing is sent to Google Maps.
  • Ordering partners such as Slice, DoorDash, Grubhub, Uber Eats, Seamless, Postmates and Caviar are separate companies. When you follow a link to order from them, their own privacy policies apply.
  • Facebook links take you to our page, which is governed by Meta's policies.

Emails and unsubscribing

Every marketing email we send includes a way to unsubscribe. You can also call us at 610-694-9112 or tell us in person and we'll take you off the list.

How long we keep it

We keep mailing list information until you unsubscribe. Catering request details are kept only as long as we need them to plan and complete your order and for normal business records.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we have about you. Depending on where you live, including under Pennsylvania law and other state privacy laws, you may have additional rights. To make a request, call or visit us. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Keeping it safe

This site is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections and uses modern browser security protections. Forms include spam protection. No method of sending data over the internet is perfect, so please don't send sensitive information like card numbers through our forms.

Children

This site is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page.
